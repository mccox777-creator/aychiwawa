Aychiwawa Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

Aychiwawa is a pet care game for kids. We built it to collect nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Aychiwawa does not collect, store, or share any personal information from anyone, including children. There are no accounts, sign-ins, ads, analytics, tracking, crash reporting, chat, sharing, or in-app purchases.

What stays on the device

The game saves your progress, like your dog's name, points, and settings, in a file on your own device. That file never leaves the device, and we can't see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Internet

The game works fully offline and makes no network connections.

Photos

Photo mode takes pictures inside the game. They're kept in the game on your device, and Aychiwawa never uploads them.

Children's privacy

Aychiwawa is made for children and follows the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Because it collects no personal information, there is nothing for a parent to review or delete. Settings for grown-ups are behind a grown-up check in the app.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
